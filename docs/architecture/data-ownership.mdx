---
title: Data ownership
description: The contract between Omniflow and Remnawave.
---

## Remnawave owns

- VPN users and technical status
- Traffic and device usage
- Access links and subscription configuration
- Nodes, hosts, and squads

## Omniflow owns

- Customer identities and sessions
- Plans, orders, payments, refunds, and wallet entries
- Fulfillment intent and reconciliation history
- Support tickets
- Marketing, news, campaigns, promotions, and consent
- Referrals and loyalty
- Roles, permissions, sessions, and audit history

A successful payment creates one idempotent fulfillment operation. If Remnawave is unavailable, Omniflow preserves the successful payment and retries fulfillment rather than rewriting financial history.
