---
title: "0012 — Guest purchases are gift orders of one system account"
description: Why a purchase without an account reuses the gift machinery under a guest checkout account instead of creating a customer per e-mail address
---

**Status:** Accepted.

## Context

Bedolaga sells to visitors without an account: an e-mail address, a payment,
and an activation link. Every Omniflow order belongs to a customer. Creating a
customer for each typed address would let anyone create accounts for addresses
they do not own, which the e-mail sign-in deliberately avoids, and would leave
abandoned shells behind every unpaid checkout.

## Decision

**A guest purchase is a subscription gift order whose sender is one system
account**, the guest checkout. Payment, expiry, refunds, and the claim are the
existing gift paths. The activation code is mailed after payment and redeemed
by a signed-in customer; signing in by e-mail with the paying address is what
"activate without a password" means here. No customer exists until somebody who
controls an address signs in.

Landing pages with their own analytics stay declined.

## Consequences

- One customer record, the guest checkout, owns every guest order and shows in
  reports and the customer list as such.
- The code is the bearer: whoever redeems it first gets the subscription. It is
  stored sealed only until mailed.
- Guest purchases need an e-mail transport; without one they are not offered.

## Revisit when

Guest orders need per-buyer reporting (refund contact, chargeback evidence)
beyond the sealed payer address, which would argue for a dedicated buyer record.
