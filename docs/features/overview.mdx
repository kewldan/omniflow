Omniflow sells and runs access to a Remnawave VPN: a Telegram bot and a web
cabinet for customers, an operator panel for you and your team, and the
payments, renewals, referrals, and support around them. Remnawave keeps doing
what it does — users, nodes, traffic — and Omniflow never touches its database.

Each guide in this section answers three questions: what the feature gives the
business, what the customer sees, and where you turn it on. The deep reference
pages each guide links to explain the exact rules.

<Tip>
  New installation? Start with the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) and
  [Your first sale](/getting-started/first-sale), then come back here to switch on
  what your business needs. Almost everything beyond a plan and a payment
  provider is off until you turn it on.
</Tip>

## What every feature has in common

A few rules hold everywhere, and they explain most of what you will see in the
panel.

- **Money and access fail independently.** A payment is recorded even when
  Remnawave is down, and access is provisioned when it comes back. Nothing a
  customer paid for is lost to an outage.
- **Money moves only through the ledger.** Balance, referral rewards,
  withdrawals, refunds, and bonuses are ledger entries, so every figure in the
  panel can be traced to the order or action behind it.
- **Prices are versioned.** Editing a plan publishes a new version; customers who
  bought an old one keep what they paid for.
- **Destructive actions ask for a reason** and land in the audit log with the
  operator who did them.
- **Automation suggests, people decide** wherever a decision affects a customer:
  risk signals, account-sharing signals, withdrawal scores, and AI drafts never
  act on their own.
- **Optional means off.** Dormant-customer handling, AI, plan switching with
  credit for the unused remainder, and other features that change how money or
  data moves stay off until you turn them on in **Settings** or the area's own
  screen.
