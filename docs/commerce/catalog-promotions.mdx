---
title: Catalog and promotions
description: Immutable pricing snapshots and atomic promotion limits.
---

Plans use stable lowercase codes and Russian/English localizations. Published plan versions are immutable and capture duration, billing period, traffic allowance, device limit, Remnawave squads, recurrence support, and explicit upgrade, downgrade, and cancellation policies.

Prices are integer minor units paired with an uppercase ISO currency. An order references both the stable plan and exact plan version and also stores a snapshot, so changing the visible catalog cannot change historical purchases.

Promotion and promo-code rows are locked in a serializable transaction before redemption counts are checked. Global, per-code, and per-customer limits are therefore atomic. Promo input is trimmed and uppercased before lookup. Operators should also configure Valkey rate limits at the caller boundary before exposing promo entry in v0.4.
