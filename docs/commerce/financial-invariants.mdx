---
title: Financial invariants
description: Rules that all commerce code and migrations must preserve.
---

The following are release-blocking invariants:

1. Money is an integer minor-unit amount plus one uppercase three-letter currency.
2. An order satisfies `wallet + external = subtotal - discount`.
3. Discount cannot exceed subtotal; refunded amount cannot exceed paid amount.
4. A ledger transaction sums to zero independently for every currency.
5. A wallet balance is derived from append-only entries and cannot become negative.
6. Pending-order wallet reservations are subtracted from available balance and rechecked before settlement.
7. A provider event, order mutation, refund, ledger reference, and fulfillment operation is idempotent.
8. Duplicate, mismatched, overpaid, and underpaid provider events never grant an entitlement; an exact late payment can grant at most one.
9. Payment success and durable fulfillment intent commit together.
10. Provider history, consent, audit, ledger, webhook, and fulfillment history is append-only.
11. Secrets, subscription links, contact plaintext, and raw authorization headers never enter logs or operational history.
12. Refund mutations are serialized per payment intent, and pending plus successful refunds cannot exceed the provider-paid amount before the provider is called.

Focused domain tests cover wallet-first splitting, payment replay, currency mismatch, promotion scope, ledger balancing, webhook signatures, and customer lifecycle guards. PostgreSQL-backed transaction tests remain a v0.5 Testcontainers gate.
