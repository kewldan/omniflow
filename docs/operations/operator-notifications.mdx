---
title: Operator notifications
description: The Telegram group, the topics the bot creates itself, the buttons that act from the chat, the startup report, what those messages deliberately omit, and the caps that keep them readable
---

An operator supplies one thing: a Telegram group. The bot creates a topic for every
notice stream inside it, saves each topic's thread ID in the routing by itself, keeps them
bound, and re-creates one that was deleted. Nobody types a thread ID.

A notice is laid out the way the previous bot laid it out: a short header in capitals, one
line per fact with an emoji and a bold label, the time, and buttons — the customer's
Telegram, their card in the panel, the order, and the few actions that are safe to take
from a chat. Plain HTML only: no quotes, no mixed fonts.

The stream is deliberately a signal rather than a data store. A notice names the event,
the amounts, the customer's Telegram name and handle, and identifiers an operator can open
in the panel — never a subscription link, a token, or a payment payload. The one piece of
customer text it carries is a support ticket's subject and message, because the group is
where the desk reads it; the support section can turn that off. A Telegram group is a
place messages are forwarded from, screenshotted, and kept for years, so the design
question was never "what would be convenient here" but "what is safe to leave in a chat
indefinitely".

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the group">
    Create a Telegram group and enable **Topics** in its settings. Topics are a supergroup
    feature; a plain group cannot hold them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the bot">
    Add the bot and grant it **Manage Topics** and **Send Messages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point the bot at it">
    Enter the group's ID in **Settings → Integrations → Operator group → Operator chat ID**
    and save. A supergroup ID is negative and begins with `-100`. Forwarding a message from
    the group to a bot such as `@userinfobot` is the usual way to read it.

    `APP_OPERATOR_CHAT_ID` sets the same value from the environment; the panel's value wins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the topics appear">
    No restart is needed. Within ten seconds the bot creates one topic per stream and saves
    each thread ID. The **Topics in the operator group** card under the section shows every
    stream, its status, its thread ID, and why a topic is missing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the `bot` process delivers notices. The `api` and `worker` processes write them into
the queue, so an installation running no bot still accumulates the record of what
happened — it simply has nobody to tell.

## The topics

| Topic | Kind | Carries | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 💳 Purchases | `purchase` | Settled subscription orders | Order settlement |
| ♻️ Renewals | `renewal` | Settled extensions and renewals | Order settlement |
| 👛 Wallet top-ups | `topup` | Credited top-ups, including over- and underpayments | Top-up settlement |
| ↩️ Refunds | `refund` | Recorded refunds | Refund recording |
| ⚠️ Fulfillment failures | `fulfillment_failure` | Provisioning runs that stopped being a blip, digital-goods delivery failures, and add-ons that settled with no live entitlement | Fulfillment worker, goods worker, settlement |
| 🚨 Incidents | `incident` | Payments that need a look: a duplicate, under- or overpayment, currency mismatch, or wallet shortfall that left money at the provider with the order open, and a payment that settled an order the customer had cancelled; a chargeback, a refund whose outcome at the provider is unknown, and a refunded top-up the wallet no longer holds; a digital-goods provider out of balance, and a goods delivery that arrived after an operator had already resolved the order; and every maintenance transition, automatic or manual | Payment settlement, refunds and reversals, the goods delivery worker, the api's maintenance controller |
| 💾 Backups | `backup` | Backup outcomes — completed with its size, or failed with the error code — and restore outcomes | The process that took or restored the backup |
| 🔐 Admin security | `security` | Operator sign-ins from a new address, password changes and resets, two-factor removal, and owner grants | Operator identity service |
| 💬 Support tickets | `support` | Every new support ticket, and every later customer message in a ticket (`event: customer_reply`), from the bot or the web panel. The notice shows the ticket's subject and the customer's message, read from the database when it is sent, unless the support section's **Show the ticket's subject and message in the group** is off | Database triggers on `support_tickets` and `support_messages` |
| 📣 Campaign previews | `campaign_test` | One copy of a campaign's message, asked for before the audience is committed to | Campaign test send |
| 🔔 Notice previews | `notice_test` | One rendered copy of a transactional notice an operator is rewording | Notice test send |
| 🖥 Remnawave nodes | `node` | Remnawave's own node, panel-service, and infrastructure-billing webhook events, as the event name and the node's name | [Remnawave webhooks](/integrations/remnawave#webhooks) |
| 📈 Usage monitoring | `monitoring` | Torrent-blocker reports, and the monitoring passes' findings | Remnawave webhooks, the worker's monitoring jobs |
| 📈 Anomalies | `anomaly` | A raised anomaly signal: the metric, severity, observed value, and threshold — never the subject | The anomaly evaluator |
| 🎁 Trials | `trial` | Every trial claimed, as its order | A trigger on `trial_claims` |
| ➕ Add-ons | `addon` | Every add-on settled onto a subscription | A trigger on the `addon.purchased` outbox event |
| 🏷 Promo codes | `promo` | Every promo code redeemed at checkout, with the discount | A trigger on `promo_redemptions` |
| 🤝 Partners | `partner` | Every referral reward granted, with the role, amount, and currency | A trigger on `referral_rewards` |
| 🐞 Errors | `error` | A new application error, and the 10th, 100th, 1000th recurrence of one: the process, a fingerprint, and the count — never the message | The error log in each process |
| 🛠 Infrastructure | `infrastructure` | The bot's [startup report](#the-startup-report); a newer release on the update feed | The bot at start; the worker every six hours when `APP_UPDATE_FEED_URL` is set |
| 📊 Reports | `report` | Scheduled daily, weekly, and monthly summaries: new customers, trials, paid orders, top-ups, revenue per currency, tickets opened and waiting, active subscriptions | The worker, per the reports section |
| 🎯 Loyalty and referrals | `marketing` | Every loyalty tier change (`event: loyalty_tier`) with the customer identifier, the tier codes it moved from and to, and the reason — `evaluation` or `operator_override` | Database trigger on `loyalty_standing_history` |

The two preview kinds (`campaign_test`, `notice_test`) carry something the others deliberately do not: a rendered message body. Everything else here is a set of allowlisted, non-personal fields, and a preview is by definition the copy itself. Both say plainly that nobody else received them — a preview mistaken for a real send is somebody reaching for the pause button. See [Campaigns](/commerce/campaigns) and [Message wording](/operations/message-wording).

Payment anomalies are most of what the incidents topic carries. Each notice names the
order, the provider, the operation, the classification, the currency and amount, and the
order's state at the time, and is deduplicated per payment intent and classification — a
replayed webhook adds nothing. Refund, chargeback, and goods-delivery incidents are
deduplicated the same way, per refund, per reported reversal, per order, or per provider
and hour. What each classification means and how it is resolved is in
[Orders and payments](/commerce/orders-payments#when-a-payment-does-not-settle).
Maintenance is detected in the `api` process, which has no Telegram client, so the api
writes each transition into the same `operator_notifications` queue the bot drains; one
transition is one message, deduplicated per direction and minute.

Backup and restore outcomes are queued the same way by the process that ran them, keyed on
the backup and the outcome, so a retried job reports once. The `backups` table stays the
record: a notice that cannot be queued is logged and never fails the backup. See [Maintenance mode](/operations/maintenance-mode)
and [Backup and restore](/operations/backup-restore).

Support notices also announce an overdue first response once per ticket
(`event: sla_breach`, with the queue, priority, and minutes overdue) whenever a queue has a
first-response target, unless the support section switches `notifySlaBreach` off. Each
stream queued by a trigger stays quiet inside a Bedolaga import, so migrating a customer
base does not replay its history into the group. The error log itself — messages, counts,
first and last seen — is on `/admin/system` behind `system.read`.

## Configuration

| Variable | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `APP_OPERATOR_CHAT_ID` | `0` | The group. `0` disables delivery entirely. |
| `APP_OPERATOR_NOTIFICATION_CAP` | `30` | Messages sent per kind per window before the rest are suppressed. `0` removes the cap. |
| `APP_OPERATOR_NOTIFICATION_WINDOW_MINUTES` | `5` | The rolling window the cap counts over. A zero or unparseable value falls back to five minutes. |

All three are fallbacks. The `operator_group` section on `/admin/settings/integrations`
overrides them on the next pass, without a restart: the chat (changing it rebinds every
topic in the new group), topics or one flat chat, the notice language, who may use the
notice buttons, the cap and its window, and which streams are switched off. Quiet hours live in the alerting section and hold non-urgent
notices pending until the window ends, counted in the branding time zone. See
[Installation settings](/operations/installation-settings).

## When binding fails

A topic that cannot be created is recorded as `failed` on `operator_topics` with a
classified reason rather than silently doing nothing. The raw Telegram message is dropped
because it can echo request content.

| `last_error_code` | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| `missing_manage_topics_permission` | Grant the bot Manage Topics |
| `forum_topics_disabled` | Enable Topics in the group settings |
| `chat_not_found` | Wrong chat ID, or the group was upgraded to a supergroup and its ID changed |
| `bot_not_in_group` | Re-add the bot |
| `telegram_error` | Anything else; read the bot log for context |

The **Topics in the operator group** card shows the reason in words. Fix the cause and
press **Recreate topics**: every topic goes back to `pending`, and the bot creates fresh
ones on its next pass and saves their thread IDs. The old topics stay in the group with
their history; close or delete them in Telegram. The request is audited as
`panel.operator_topics.recreate_requested`. Without the panel, the next notification of a
kind re-attempts its binding before it sends.

A failed topic is not retried on its own every pass, so a missing right does not turn into
a request to Telegram every ten seconds. New streams added by an upgrade get their topics
on the next pass.

Changing `APP_OPERATOR_CHAT_ID` rebinds everything. Rows whose stored chat no longer
matches have their topic identifier cleared and their status reset to `pending`, so fresh
topics are created in the new group rather than messages being sent into a group you have
left.

A topic deleted in Telegram is re-created on the next message to it, once, and the message
is then retried.

## Delivery

The notifier drains up to 50 pending notices every ten seconds, in creation order, inside
one transaction with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` so two bots cannot deliver the same row.
Each notice ends in exactly one terminal state:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | Queued, not yet attempted. Rows stay here indefinitely while no group is configured. |
| `sent` | Delivered, with `sent_at` recorded. |
| `suppressed` | The volume cap was reached; `error_code = volume_cap`. |
| `failed` | Telegram refused, with a classified `error_code`. |

A `failed` notice is not retried. The row and its payload remain in the database, so the
record of the event survives even when the message did not, but the fix for a persistent
failure is to correct the group and let the next event through rather than to expect a
replay.

## Volume control

```bash
APP_OPERATOR_NOTIFICATION_CAP=30
APP_OPERATOR_NOTIFICATION_WINDOW_MINUTES=5
```

Before each send the notifier counts how many notices of that kind were **sent** inside
the window. At or above the cap, the notice is completed as `suppressed` with reason
`volume_cap` and a warning is logged with the kind and the number suppressed in that pass.

The cap is per kind, so a Remnawave outage producing thousands of fulfillment failures
cannot bury the purchase stream, and suppressed notices do not themselves count towards
the cap. Nothing is lost: the rows stay in the database with their payloads.

Set the cap to `0` to send everything.

<Warning>
  A group configured for the first time after a long run drains a backlog. The queue keeps
  every notice written while no group existed, and the drain is bounded to 50 every ten
  seconds with the volume cap applied on top — so most of a large backlog is delivered as
  `suppressed` rather than as messages. That is the intended outcome; read the history in
  the panel rather than expecting it in the chat.
</Warning>

## Deduplication

Every notice carries a dedupe key: the order for a purchase, the refund for a refund, the
fulfillment operation for a failure, the event and account for a security notice.
`operator_notifications (kind, dedupe_key)` is unique and the insert is
`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, so a retried webhook, a second reconciliation pass, and a job
that fails repeatedly all produce one message.

The notice is queued inside the same transaction as the event it describes. A notice
therefore exists exactly when the thing it reports is durable — never for an event that
rolled back, and never missing for one that committed. A queue failure is logged and never
fails the transaction that caused it, because a settled payment must not be undone by a
chat message.

## What a notice contains

Each kind and event has its own template: an emoji header in capitals that says what
happened, one line per fact with a bold label — the amounts as money in the installation's
currency — and the time in the installation's timezone. Panel buttons (the customer's
profile, the order, the ticket) appear when `APP_PUBLIC_URL` is an `https://` address; the
✉️ DM button appears when the customer has a public Telegram username. **Notice language**
(`locale`, English or Russian) in the operator group settings chooses the language, and
topics created after it was chosen are named in it too. A new ticket reads like this:

```text
🎫 НОВЫЙ ТИКЕТ

🆔 ID: 0d9b1f2c
👤 Пользователь: G
🆔 Telegram ID: 8792980158
📱 Username: @ranalda16
📂 Очередь: Общие вопросы
📝 Заголовок: Процент за друга
📩 Сообщение:
Я пришёл по рефералке друга…
📅 Создан: 20 ноя, 21:31 MSK

[✉️ ЛС] [👤 Профиль]
[💬 Открыть тикет]
[🔒 Закрыть тикет]
[🚫 Заблокировать]
```

A wallet top-up names who paid, how much, how, and the balance before and after:

```text
💰 ПОПОЛНЕНИЕ БАЛАНСА

👤 Пользователь: Иван
🆔 Telegram ID: 777000
📱 Username: @ivan
💵 Сумма: +500 ₽
💳 Способ: YooKassa · СБП
💼 Баланс: 250 ₽ → 750 ₽
🔁 Оплата: 3-я
🧾 Заказ: f38ec162
📅 Время: 22 ноя, 00:52 MSK

[✉️ ЛС] [👤 Профиль]
[🧾 Заказ]
```

The payload is still a fixed allowlist — anything else a payload happens to hold is dropped
rather than filtered, so a field added in future cannot leak by omission:

```text
orderId · subscriptionId · entitlementId · provider · operation · currency
amountMinor · creditedMinor · classification · reason · source · errorCode
fileName · sizeBytes · status · event · adminUserId · method · ticketId · queueId
signalId · metric · severity · observed · threshold · subjectType
process · fingerprint · occurrences · version · previousVersion · releaseUrl
overdueMinutes · priority
```

At delivery the bot reads the records those identifiers name: the customer's Telegram name,
ID, and handle, their account status, and how many payments they have made, counting a
previous bot's; the plan and period an order bought, the provider and method that settled
it, the wallet part, the discount and promo code; the wallet balance after a top-up; a
ticket's queue, priority, subject, and the customer message the notice is about (clipped
at 800 characters). A record that cannot be read is left out and the notice still goes. A
subscription link, a provider token, or a payment payload never reaches the group. A
notice no template knows lists its allowlisted fields in words.

A security notice names the event, the operator account, and the method, and nothing else.
An operator's address, a session token, and a client IP stay out of the group chat; that
detail lives in the audit trail, behind the permissions that govern it. See
[Admin panel](/operations/admin-panel).

## Buttons that act from the chat

Some notices carry buttons that do something rather than open a page:

| Button | On | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔒 Close the ticket | New tickets, customer replies, overdue first replies | Closes the ticket; the customer gets the same "closed" message as from the panel |
| 🚫 Block | Support notices, torrent-blocker reports | Asks "Yes, block / Cancel" first, then suspends the customer |
| ✅ Unblock | The same notices, for a customer who is blocked | Reactivates the customer |
| 🔁 Retry now | A delivery that is retrying | Puts the failed provisioning operation back in the queue |

Each tap is checked before anything changes:

- it came from the configured operator group, not from another chat the notice was forwarded to;
- the person who pressed it may act there: one of the Telegram IDs in the operator group
  section's **Who may use notice buttons**, or, when that is empty, in
  `APP_OPERATOR_TELEGRAM_IDS`; with neither set, the group's owner and administrators;
- the same tap was not already handled.

The action runs through the panel's own service, with `telegram:<id>` as the actor, so a
close or a block from the chat writes the same audit entry, customer lifecycle event, and
customer message as one made in the panel. A refused tap is audited too, as
`telegram.notice_action.denied` with outcome `denied`. Every action is idempotent: a second
tap on a closed ticket or a blocked customer changes nothing and says so. After an action
the button changes to show what happened and who did it, for example `🔒 Closed · Anna`.

The buttons need `APP_DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, which the panel's service is built with. Without
it notices carry links only.

## The startup report

When the bot starts it posts one summary to the infrastructure topic:

```text
🚀 Omniflow · v1.4.1
Запущен и готов к работе

⚠️ Требует внимания
• Тикетов ждут ответа: 1

👥 Пользователи
Всего: 2 894
Новых за сутки: +1

💳 Активные подписки
Платных: 240
Пробных: 5

💰 Деньги
Пополнения за сутки: 1 518 ₽
На балансах: 4 313 ₽

🛠 Система
Remnawave: 🟢 на связи · 341 мс
Тикетов открыто: 1
Продажи: открыты для всех
```

**Needs attention** lists tickets waiting for the desk, a Remnawave panel that does not
answer, failed deliveries in the last seven days, and maintenance mode; with none of those it
says all is well. The figures are counts and sums only, read once at start: active
customers; active and limited subscriptions on paid plans and on trials; wallet top-ups
paid in the last 24 hours and the total on customer balances, per currency. The Remnawave
line is one timed request to the panel. **Sales** is the access section's mode. The report
goes through the same volume cap and quiet hours as every other notice.

## Fulfillment failure notices

A failing provisioning run does not notify immediately. Ordinary backoff is expected to
recover, so an operator is told only after three failed attempts, or immediately on a
permanent failure. The dedupe key is the operation, so one struggling entitlement produces
one message however many times it retries.

The notice carries the customer, the operation, the current status, and a classified error
code such as `remnawave_http_502` or `remnawave_unavailable`, with a **Retry now** button
while the operation is retrying. It never carries the panel's response body.

Two related cases land in the same topic:

- A **digital-goods delivery** that the provider could not complete.
- An **add-on that settled with no live entitlement** — the order keeps its paid state so
  the money is never silently lost, and the notice, together with the
  `addon.no_active_entitlement` audit event, tells an operator exactly what to refund or
  re-apply.
