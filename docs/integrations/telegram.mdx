---
title: Telegram bot
description: Telegram-first customer experience and webhook delivery.
---

The bot provides a localized Russian and English self-service experience:

- Automatic account linking through the exact Telegram ID stored in Remnawave
- Subscription status, remaining days, expiry, and traffic progress
- Protected open/copy subscription actions and a short connection guide
- Device count, friendly names, per-device removal, and remove-all with confirmation
- Subscription-link rotation with a warning that existing clients must reconnect
- Persisted language and expiry/traffic notification preferences
- Idempotent expiry alerts at 7, 3, 1, and 0 days and traffic alerts at 80% and 100%
- Referral deep links, immutable attribution, and invited-user counts
- In-bot support requests persisted for the operator workspace
- Inline refresh, back navigation, retry states, and configurable support handoff

Use `/start` or `/menu` in a private chat. Messages containing account data are protected against forwarding and disable link previews. Callback queries are acknowledged before backend work so Telegram controls remain responsive.

If no matching Remnawave user exists, the bot does not guess or permit an insecure self-link. It sends the customer to the configured support contact.

`/settings`, `/support`, and `/cancel` are available in addition to `/start` and `/menu`. Support compose state expires after 30 minutes, and every customer has at most one open ticket.

Plan purchase, renewal, wallet usage, and rewards remain payment-provider and policy dependent. The bot does not present a fake checkout until an administrator configures those capabilities.

The current bot uses long polling. A secret-token-validated webhook mode will be added before the production deployment milestone.
